ABOUT ME

Behind the camera, I'm all about people and good energy.

I enjoy capturing real moments — the laughs, the action, the in-between bits and the stuff that actually makes a day memorable.

MY STYLE

Relaxed, bright and people-focused.

Kdog's Photography started with photographing the people and events around me. Since then I've photographed sport, uni events, live music, couples and fun runs.

I like shoots to feel easygoing rather than overly staged. The goal is to make photos that still feel like you and still feel like the day they came from.
